How to connect a webhook
  1. Create a free Make.com account.
  2. Import the ready-made scenario below (fastest), or build it yourself with a Custom Webhook module + a Discord Make an API Call module.
  3. Open the Webhook module in your scenario and copy its URL.
  4. Paste that URL into the webhook field above, along with your channel ID.
  5. Turn the scenario ON in Make.com, then hit Send to Webhook here to test it.
